    The process

    How it works

    1. 📷

      Start auto scan

      Open your camera and point it at a plant or leaf.

    2. 🔍

      Searching for plant

      The camera view is continuously checked for a plant or leaf.

    3. 🌿

      Plant detected

      Once found, the clearest frame is captured automatically.

    4. ✅

      Image quality check

      The capture is checked for blur, darkness, or overexposure before analysis.

    5. 🧠

      AI analysis

      The leaf's condition is analyzed: healthy, dry, dead, or diseased.

    6. 📊

      Disease and severity

      If diseased, see the disease name, confidence, and severity.

    7. 💊

      Treatment guidance

      Get the recommended pesticide, dosage, and application frequency — only when a real infection is found.

    About this project

    An independent AI agriculture assistant

    PlantCare AI is built around one idea: point your camera at a leaf and let the system do the rest. It continuously scans the camera view, waits until it's confident a plant or leaf is actually present, automatically captures a clear frame, and only then analyzes the leaf's condition.

    Healthy, dry, and dead leaves are treated as distinct outcomes from disease — pesticide is only ever recommended for a genuine, treatable infection, never for a leaf that's already healthy or already dead.

    The current build uses a two-stage colour-pattern heuristic (plant presence, then condition), with an architecture designed so a real trained detection and classification model can be dropped into either stage for production-grade accuracy.
